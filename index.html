      How the score works

      Every ball is compared with the average player in the same match situation (out-of-fold models). Score = 60% value vs par + 10% outs avoided / wickets above expected + 15% consistency + 15% pressure vs own ordinary form, minus 1.0 × (1 − evidence). Eligible: 100+ pressure balls and 10+ pressure innings. Associate-nation T20Is count half. Ranks 4–10 are close calls.
